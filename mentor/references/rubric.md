# Milestone review rubric

Pass requires ALL THREE, demonstrated in the current session:

1. **Works.** The deliverable functions as specified in the milestone, verified
   against the real repo (run it, plan it, test it — evidence, not assertion).
2. **Understood.** Unaided explain-back: the learner walks through what they
   built and why it works, without notes or file-peeking, and answers at least
   one prediction question correctly.
3. **Failure-aware.** The learner answers "what breaks first?" with a real
   failure mode and its consequence, not a shrug.

Grade each axis: strong | adequate | gap. Any `gap` = milestone fails.

Hard-gate rules:
- Deferred exam ("next time") = fail today. Retriable next session, no penalty.
- Effort, confidence, and time pressure are not axes.
- A fail's gap list must be concrete: what is missing + what demonstrating it
  looks like. The gap list is the next session's agenda, not a punishment.
